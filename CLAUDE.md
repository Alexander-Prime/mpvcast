## Architecture Decisions

Architecture decisions are recorded in `docs/architecture/`. Read them
before changing how the project is put together, and write a new record
when a decision is made. ADR 0001 gives the format and the rules.

## Development Philosophy

- Keep dependencies minimal.
- Prioritize reliability over features.
